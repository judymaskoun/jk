SAFE UPDATE — Instructions

1. Unzip this archive.
2. Copy the website files into the same relative folders in your existing GitHub repository.
3. Commit the changes, then refresh the GitHub Pages website.

This patch does NOT replace your assets/style.css, assets/site.js, assets/content.js,
existing content/sections.json, or any other existing Pages CMS content.

Pages CMS controls added:
  Section visibility (show / hide)
    Home — publication / talks / awards cards: OFF by default
    Research — interest bubbles: ON by default
  Home — three statistics cards
    Edit numbers, labels, link URLs, order, and per-card visibility
  Research — interest bubbles
    Edit each bubble's text, order, and per-bubble visibility

Courses remains on the Teaching page as the section balloon label.
The footer profile links are restored on CV and Contact only, while the upper
parts of those two pages continue to have no profile icons.

To show the three homepage cards again, go to Pages CMS > Section visibility,
turn ON the homepage statistics control, and save.

If you previously customized your .pages.yml, merge the new collections and
visibility fields instead of overwriting that file.
