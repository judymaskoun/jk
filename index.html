About Me

My background and research

Background

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Alberta, where I work on water and wastewater treatment research. I hold a PhD in Environmental Engineering from the University of Alberta, an MSc in Materials Science and Nanotechnology from Bilkent University, and a BSc in Chemical Engineering from Middle East Technical University.

Research focus

My research interests lie at the intersection of advanced oxidation processes, adsorbent materials, and applied environmental engineering. I develop biochar-supported photocatalysts, investigate contaminant removal and toxicity in real water matrices, and work on nature-based and pilot-scale systems for practical treatment applications.
