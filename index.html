About Me

My background and research

Background

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Alberta. I hold a PhD in Environmental Engineering from the University of Alberta, an MSc in Materials Science and Nanotechnology from Bilkent University, and a BSc in Chemical Engineering from Middle East Technical University.

Research focus

My research interests lie at the intersection of advanced oxidation processes, adsorption, and nature-based solutions for water and wastewater treatment. I work on the development of advanced materials and pilot-scale systems for practical treatment applications.
